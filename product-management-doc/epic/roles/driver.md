# Role: Driver

## Role Intent

Execute active trips and route operations in the field with a focused transport-only workflow.

## Current Access Surface

- Driver console
- Profile

## Current Capabilities

- Access active trip information.
- Start and end trips.
- Submit GPS updates.
- Handle halt, delay, and SOS events.
- Manage onboard/offboard actions relevant to trip execution.
- Complete stop-level actions during a trip.
- Access transport AI/status prompts related to current transport actions.

## Backend Capability References

- `school-operations-service`
- transport trip endpoints

## Product Constraints / Notes

- Driver experience should stay narrow and operational.
- Any future changes should prefer low-friction, high-clarity actions suitable for field use.
