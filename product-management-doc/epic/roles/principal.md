# Role: Principal

## Role Intent

Act as a leadership operator with broad school visibility across academics, operations, attendance, communication, transport, and analytics.

## Current Access Surface

- Dashboard
- School CMS
- Routing
- Students
- Teachers
- Admissions
- Class
- Department / academic structure
- Attendance
- Examinations
- Timetable
- HR leaves
- Transport hub
- Library
- Forum
- Communication
- Billing and fees
- School analytics
- LUMINA Studio
- Profile

## Current Capabilities

- Access most school-admin-style school management surfaces.
- Review student, teacher, class, and departmental data.
- Use attendance, timetable, exams, communication, and analytics workflows.
- Access school CMS and school operational visibility tools.
- Participate in HR leave approval and operational oversight workflows.
- Access transport and school intelligence surfaces when feature-gated modules are enabled.

## Product Constraints / Notes

- Current implementation largely groups principal with school-admin/manager access in the UI.
- If principal flows need to diverge later, this document is the right place to split approval, oversight, and edit permissions.
