# Role: School Admin

## Role Intent

Operate the school as the primary administrative owner across admissions, academics, operations, finance, communication, and school-facing intelligence.

## Current Access Surface

- Dashboard
- School CMS
- Routing
- Students
- Teachers
- Admissions
- Class
- Department / academic structure
- Attendance
- Examinations
- Timetable
- HR leaves
- Transport hub
- Library
- Forum
- Communication
- Billing and fees
- School analytics
- LUMINA Studio
- Profile

## Current Capabilities

- Log in to the school workspace using school credentials.
- Create and manage admissions, including enrollment flows.
- Create, update, and manage students.
- Create and manage teacher accounts and related user records.
- Manage departments, subjects, classes, and academic assignments.
- Access timetable creation, bulk loading, and optimization flows.
- Manage attendance dashboards and teacher marking workflows.
- Manage exams, schedules, marks, and result publishing flows.
- Manage school public-site content through school CMS.
- Manage school routing/domain-related school-side workflows.
- View and manage finance/billing surfaces and fee-related flows.
- Use transport operations hub when transport features are enabled.
- Publish announcements and access communication center workflows.
- Access library, forum, and analytics surfaces.
- Use AI/LUMINA operational assistance.
- Participate in HR leave approval workflows.

## Product Constraints / Notes

- This is the most important day-to-day operator role.
- Any future flow changes here should be evaluated for system-of-record integrity first, then UX convenience second.
