# Role: Staff

## Role Intent

Provide a lightweight administrative/support role with access to leave workflows and any future school support operations.

## Current Access Surface

- HR leaves

## Current Capabilities

- Access leave request workflows.
- Create leave requests.
- View own leave history/status.

## Backend Capability References

- `school-operations-service`
- HR management endpoints

## Product Constraints / Notes

- Staff access appears intentionally narrow in the current implementation.
- This is a good role to extend later if non-teaching operations workflows are introduced.
